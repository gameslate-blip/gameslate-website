About GameSlate

Game discovery, in one place.

GameSlate is an Android app for discovering PC games. It brings together game information and selected community and platform data so you can make a more informed choice about what to play next.

GameSlate does not require an account. Your favorites are stored locally on your device.

Privacy policy

Your data, clearly explained.

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Verification note: This policy describes the app behavior and SDK configuration currently verified by the developer. AdMob account-level Privacy & Messaging configuration and some third-party data-retention details have not yet been independently verified. This page must be reviewed and updated before being treated as a final legal policy.

1. No account required

GameSlate does not offer account creation, sign-up, or login. It does not operate a GameSlate account system. Favorites are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to GameSlate servers. You can remove this device-local data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. This is not an account-deletion process because GameSlate accounts do not exist.

2. Game discovery data and providers

To provide game discovery features, the app communicates with third-party services. Depending on the feature you use, this can include:

  • Steam Store: search, app details, featured categories, and similar games.
  • Steam Web API: current player counts and review summaries.
  • SteamData.dev: app feeds, trending information, recent releases, and player history.
  • ProtonDB: Linux compatibility summaries and community reports.
  • Steam image CDN: game header artwork.

Search terms are sent to Steam. Game IDs and feed requests are sent to the relevant data providers to retrieve requested information. SteamDB, SteamData.dev, and ProtonDB pages are opened only when you tap a link to them. These providers process information under their own privacy terms and practices.

3. Advertising and consent

GameSlate uses Google Mobile Ads SDK version 25.5.0 and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK version 4.0.0. Release builds use the app's AdMob identifiers; debug builds use Google's demo identifiers.

At launch, the app refreshes consent information through UMP and presents a consent form when required. GameSlate requests ads only when UMP's canRequestAds() returns true. When UMP indicates that a privacy-options entry point is required, the app displays an “Ad privacy choices” link that opens UMP's privacy form.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK disclosure says the SDK may collect and share IP address, app interactions, diagnostics, and identifiers such as Android Advertising ID and App Set ID for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. IP address may be used to estimate general location. The Android release manifest includes advertising ID and Ad Services permissions related to advertising ID, attribution, and topics.

GameSlate does not run first-party analytics. This does not mean that the third-party advertising SDK performs no analytics-related processing.

AdMob account configuration: The developer has not yet verified which Privacy & Messaging messages are configured or published in the AdMob account, which apps are selected, or their geographic targeting. Do not interpret this page as confirmation that those account settings have been completed.

4. Data retention and security

Favorites remain on your device unless you clear app data or uninstall the app. Data handled by third-party providers is subject to their respective retention and security practices. The developer has not yet verified all third-party retention periods, so no specific retention duration is promised here.
